About Tru

I'm a kid who makes games, art, and stuff. Based in Brooklyn, NY.

I started making micro-games in Scratch when I was in middle school. The characters I drew for the games started showing up on real things — pouches, mugs, keychains, t-shirts. Now I make both: games you can play and things you can hold.

Be Tru Maker is where I share what I know — how to build games, how to turn your art into real products, and what happens when you actually try.
