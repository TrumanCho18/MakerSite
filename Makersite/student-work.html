Five days of making. Each kid sketched a character, drew it in Procreate, swapped it into one of Tru's games, and printed it on a t-shirt to take home.

Workshop 2 — Making Shirts with Procreate

A two-hour session for our youngest makers. Each kid picked their favourite BeTru characters, laid out a design, learned to write in Procreate, and made vinyl decals. Truman heat-pressed each one onto a t-shirt to wear home. Kids also got a notebook with stickers to decorate.
