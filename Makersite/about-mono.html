About

I'm Truman Cho, a senior at Brooklyn Technical High School in New York.

If it doesn't exist,
I make it

I have always had a drive to take things that interested me and make them my own. From games, to stories, merchandise, and clothing, I've been creating things my whole life.

When I was 4, I built my own version out of blocks when I used up my screentime. At 8, I wanted a Game Boy, so I made many cardboard versions. That's still how I work: if something doesn't exist the way I need it, I make it.
