Workshops

Turn your drawings into games you can play and objects you can hold. Start with a sketch, make it digital, drop it into a real game, and finish with your character printed on something you keep.

01

The Formats

Two ways in. Both follow the same five lessons.

In Person

Brooklyn, small groups

  • 2 hours a day, Monday to Friday
  • Typical time 10am to 12pm
  • Small groups with lots of individual attention
  • Kids going into 4th to 6th grade
  • Bring an iPad, Procreate and a digital pencil

Virtual & Self-Led

Anywhere, at your own pace

  • Mirrored lessons from in person
  • Short tutorials from Tru
  • Downloadable files for each lesson
  • Upload your artwork and sprites
  • Free decals sent to your home to DIY
02

The Lessons

Five steps, the same loop Truman uses on his own work.

Lesson 1

Create

Develop an idea

  • Overview of the maker process
  • Play test Tru's games together
  • Sketch your own character
Lesson 2

Digitize

Bring it to life

  • Continue character development
  • Basics of Procreate tools
  • Animation intro
  • Digitize your character
Lesson 3

Code

See yourself in the game

  • How Tru's games are built
  • Swap your character in
  • Make it move
Lesson 4

Play

Test it and fix it

  • Play your own game
  • Change what does not feel right
  • Share it with the group
Lesson 5

Make

Take it home

  • Print your character as a decal
  • Press it onto a shirt
  • Keep the thing you made
