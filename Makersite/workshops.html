Maker Workshops

Turn your drawings into games you can play and objects you can hold.

Hi, I'm Truman. I'm a rising senior at Brooklyn Tech, and I've been teaching myself game design, digital art, and coding for years. I offer small in-person and online/self-led workshops where I share my creative loop process. We start with an idea/sketch, make it digital, drop it into a real game, and finish with your character printed on actual products. It's real, hands-on making, built for kids going into 4th to 6th grade.

The Formats

In-person

  • 2 hours a day, Monday to Friday
  • Typical time: 10am to 12pm
  • Small groups with lots of individual attention
  • Kids going into 4th to 6th grade
  • Bring an iPad, Procreate, and a digital pencil

Virtual/self-led

  • Mirrored lessons from in person
  • Short tutorials from Tru
  • Downloadable files for each lesson
  • Upload artwork and sprites
  • Free decals sent to your home to DIY
  • Needs an iPad, Procreate, and a digital pencil

The Lessons

Lesson 1
CREATE

Develop an idea

  • Presentation overview of the maker process
  • Play test Tru's games together
  • Brainstorm session — sketch your own character
Lesson 2
DIGITIZE

Bring it to life

  • Continue character development
  • Learn the basics of Procreate tools
  • Animation intro
  • Digitize your character
Lesson 3
CODE

See yourself in the game

  • Learn the basics of how Tru's games are built
  • Swap your character into a real game
  • Play it yourself
  • See what's possible — no coding experience needed
Lesson 4
PLAY

Test and Plan

  • Play test each other's games
  • Practice coding and make improvements
  • Decide what product to make at the end
Lesson 5
MAKE

Take it home

  • Make a custom product featuring their own character
  • Shirt, tote, sticker — something real to keep
  • Show-and-tell to wrap up
