About Tru

Age 8, designing for Game Boy
Age 8, cardboard Game Boy
High school, continuing game dev

I'm Truman Cho, a senior at Brooklyn Technical High School in New York.

I have always had a drive to take things that interested me and make them my own. From games, to stories, merchandise, and clothing, I've been creating things my whole life.

When I was 4, I built my own version out of blocks when I used up my screentime. At 8, I wanted a Game Boy, so I made a cardboard one that took me nearly a year to finish. That's still how I work: if something doesn't exist the way I need it, I make it.

From publishing my own games on itch.io, selling my art on merchandise, to volunteering at my church youth group ministry, I found a process loop that helped me grow as a creative person. This platform is here to share what I have learned with kids that may be curious like I was, and even for those that may not know their own creative magic ability yet.

By offering free resources and workshops, I want kids to go from just consuming to actually making, the same way I did.
