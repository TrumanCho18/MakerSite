Children's Center · Nat Azarow

Kids making at Nat Azarow

Kids at the Nat Azarow Children's Center got hands-on making stickers and friendship bracelets for the Mayor of New York. The school requested Tru's charms to be part of the experience. Many kids have sensory sensitivity and this activity empowered them to feel proud that they were able to make something on their own.

Pop-Up · PS38 · 5th Grade

PS38 pop-up with 5th graders

A charm bar celebration for fifth graders at PS38. Each child designed their own combination for a truly custom charm and went home with QR code stickers to further explore BeTru Maker games and ideas.
